Simple Meals - Brand Guide

Brand Mission

The purpose of Simple Meals is to make eating healthy simpler for everyday people. Whether you're a college student, busy professional, parent or bodybuilder, Simple Meals has options for you. We have a wide variety of lunches and dinners to choose from and even options for breakfast and snacks.

Brand Slogan

Making eating healthy simple.

Font and Colors

- Light Blue #add8e6: Used for borders and heading background
- Dark Grey #333333 and #2f4f4f: Used for fonts (darker grey for headers
- Beige #f5f5dc: Used for overall background color
- Courier New: Used for font type on page
